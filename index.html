HistoryKampala, Uganda

A hotel born
in 2007.

Imperial Royale’s early story is tied to a major moment for Kampala.

Imperial Royale Hotel, on Kintu Road in Kampala, says it opened in the later part of 2007. That November, Uganda hosted the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting, known as CHOGM.

According to the hotel, it was designated the meeting’s “Media Hotel” and served more than 400 international journalists covering the event.

The CHOGM contract later became the subject of a legal dispute. In 2025, Uganda’s Court of Appeal upheld a ruling that the hotel had not been ready during an agreed period and ordered a refund and damages.

Today, the hotel lists guest rooms, meeting spaces and a fifth-floor pool among its facilities. It is part of the Imperial Group of Hotels.

Imperial Royale is a different property from the older Grand Imperial Hotel. Sources are on page 9.
